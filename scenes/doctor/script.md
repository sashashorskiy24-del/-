# Alex at the Doctor — script (~30 s, vertical short)

Clinic exam room. Doctor (woman, white coat) at a small desk; Alex on the exam couch holding a mug of tea with the spoon still in it.

| # | Who | Line | Action |
|---|-----|------|--------|
| 1 | DOCTOR | Hello, Alex. What seems to be the problem? | Doctor looks up from her clipboard. |
| 2 | ALEX | Every time I drink tea, my eye hurts. | Alex sips. The spoon handle pokes him in the eye. He winces. |
| 3 | DOCTOR | Have you tried taking the spoon out of the cup? | Deadpan. Points at the spoon. |
| 4 | ALEX | Doctor... you're a genius. | Pulls the spoon out, stares at it, amazed. |
| 5 | DOCTOR | Anything else? | Pen ready. |
| 6 | ALEX | Yes. When I press here, it hurts. And here. And here. | Pokes his arm, belly, head with one finger, wincing each time. |
| 7 | DOCTOR | Alex... your finger is broken. | Close-up: his finger, bent. |
| 8 | ALEX | Oh. How much do I owe you? | |
| 9 | DOCTOR | Two hundred dollars. | |
| 10 | ALEX | Can I pay with tea? | Holds the mug out hopefully. Doctor facepalms. End. |
